Key Rules

Rules used by this calculator.

  • Skilled non-UAE employee: MOHRE skill category must be 1-5 and salary must be AED 4,000 or more.
  • All UAE Nationals are generally counted as skilled employees; however, the Skilled UAE National Employees field remains editable so the calculator can follow the Tawteen report exactly.
  • Example: salary AED 20,000 + skill category 6 = Unskilled.
  • Example: salary AED 3,000 + skill category 1 = Unskilled.
  • Example: salary AED 20,000 + skill category 1 = Skilled.
  • 0-19 total employees: no Emiratization requirement.
  • 20-49 total employees: requirement is 1 UAE National for the full year. The same annual requirement applies whether reviewing H1 or H2.
  • 50 or more total employees: target is based on skilled employees.
  • The normal current-year targets are last year Emiratization percentage + 1% for H1 and last year Emiratization percentage + 2% for H2. Both H1 and H2 targets remain editable so the figures can follow the Tawteen report exactly.
  • For establishments with 50 or more employees, sensitivity is prepared for the selected half-year and compares the calculated requirement against the Total UAE Nationals working in the unit, not only Skilled UAE Nationals.
  • Branches are treated as separate units for Emiratization calculations.
  • The Tawteen report figures should be reviewed before relying on the result.

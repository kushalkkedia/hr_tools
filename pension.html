Key Rules

Summary of the pension rules used by this calculator.

  • Old registration / Law No. 1999: employee contribution remains 5%.
  • New registration under Law No. 2023: employee contribution is 11%.
  • If an employee was already registered under the old law and takes a second employment, the old registration continues and the employee contribution remains 5%.
  • Up to August 2026: employer contribution is 12.5% and Government contribution is 2.5%.
  • From September 2026: employer contribution becomes 15% and Government contribution becomes 0%.
  • Full-month pension is payable irrespective of the date of joining in that month.
  • Full-month pension is payable irrespective of the date of leaving in that month.
  • Salary is updated in the Pension Authority system every January.
  • If registration confirmation is not completed within two days by the Emirati employee, a fine of AED 200 per day may apply.
For internal estimation and reference purposes. Verify the employee's pension registration and Pension Authority records before processing.

      Rules

      The rules this calculator follows.

      For internal estimation only. These figures are an estimate and are not a final settlement. For correct figures, contact your HR department.

      The rules

      • One year of continuous service is the threshold. Below it, nothing is payable.
      • 21 days' basic pay for each of the first five years, 30 days' for each year after.
      • Basic salary only. Housing, transport, and every other allowance are excluded from the accrual.
      • Unpaid leave does not count as service.
      • Total gratuity cannot exceed two years' wage.

      Governing law

      • Federal Decree-Law No. (33) of 2021 Regulating Labour Relations
      • Cabinet Resolution No. (1) of 2022 Concerning the Executive Regulation of Federal Decree-Law No. (33) of 2021 Regulating Labour Relations
